## Why

## Rules
- One run = **one series** about the same stickman couple (same look, same names/colours in every part), **4 parts** ("PART 1/4" ... "PART 4/4" always on top).
- Each part **30-60 s** (not longer: watch time to the end matters more than length).
- The story idea comes from a relationship video with **≥ 1M views** (rule above), stretched into a gripping arc: Part 1 hook + conflict, Parts 2-3 escalation and a twist, Part 4 payoff/emotional ending.
- **Hook in the first 2 s** of every part (action, not a slow intro). Something new happens every 3-5 s.
- Every part except the last **ends on a cliffhanger**, then an end card "PART 2 → FOLLOW" (no fade-out before the open question).
- Parts 2-4 start with a **2-3 s recap** ("PREVIOUSLY ...") so new viewers get it.
- Caption: "Part N/4", the cliffhanger question, "Follow for Part N+1"; parts post in consecutive slots in order.
- Only relationship. Source rules and the virality check (≥ 1M views) as in `GENERATE.md` step 2.
- Code only, same look and rig as all reels. 1080×1920.

## Quality rules (user, 2026-11-09, after series 1)
User: "ich will dass du mehr deutlicher das machen also klarer die sätze bewegung und die nachricht dahinter ... die serie smoother machen effekte verwenden und professionell cutten den neugier von zuschauer erwecken."

**Clear sentences**
- One idea per caption, at most ~5 words ("HE GAVE HER HIS UMBRELLA."), on screen long enough to read twice.
- Speech bubbles only for the key lines (1-3 words where possible), never two bubbles at once.
- Never say in text what the picture already shows; text adds the feeling or the stakes.

**Clear movement**
- One action at a time; key poses hold ~0.5 s so the eye can read them (give, push, kneel, hug).
- Characters face whoever they react to; no crossed or tangled arms; enough space between figures.
- Every gesture is big and readable at phone size (anticipation before, settle after).

**Clear message**
- Each part has one emotional point, set up early and paid off at the end; the series message ("real love stays in the rain") is planted in Part 1 and pays off in Part 4.

**Smooth + effects**
- Eased camera moves only (no jumps), soft push-ins on emotions, quick punch-in zoom + shake on shocks.
- Effects: slow motion on the key moment, whip-pan / blur transitions between places, flash cuts on thunder, vignette and color mood per scene (cold blue = sad, warm = love), light leaks/glow on happy beats, letterbox bars for dramatic moments, freeze-frame on the cliffhanger.
- Turns are animated (no instant flips); props move with the hands.

**Professional cutting**
- Shot variety: wide (where) → medium (who) → close-up (feeling), new shot every ~2-4 s.
- Cut on action and on music hits; sound leads the picture (hear the bus before you see it).
- No dead air: every second either moves the story or builds tension.

**Follow ask (user, 2026-11-10)**
- The FOLLOW card is not a static card: the cliffhanger freeze-frame cracks open into "PART N+1 → + FOLLOW", a hand taps it, "FOLLOWING ✓" + click. Max 1.5 s, see `REFERENCE-ANALYSIS.md` → "FOLLOW ENDING + NEW MIX".

**Curiosity**
- Open with a 1-2 s flash-forward teaser of the part's biggest moment ("HOW DID IT COME TO THIS?"), then rewind.
- Captions ask questions the picture answers later; end on a freeze-frame of the open question + a short preview of the next part on the FOLLOW card.

## Steps
1. Find ≥ 1M-view relationship videos (`GENERATE.md` step 1-2), pick the one with the most gripping story.
2. Write the arc (4 parts, beat list per part, cliffhangers) to `viral/YYYY-MM-DD-series.md`; commit.
3. Build each part (`videos/NN-slug-part-N`), snap-check, render in the foreground, commit right after each part.
4. Caption per part: "Part N/4", the cliffhanger question, "Follow for Part N+1", 6-8 hashtags (+ #series #part1 ...).
5. Send each part to Telegram in order, consecutive slots (`DAILY-JOB.md`); in chat only "Das Video wurde erstellt."
6. Log in `DAILY-JOB.md` and `REFERENCE-ANALYSIS.md`, commit and push.
