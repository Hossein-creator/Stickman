# Virality check 2026-10-27 (first "generiere" run)

Rules: `GENERATE.md`. Pass = outlier ≥ 3, posted ≥ 2026-07-29 (90 days), likes + comments ≥ ~2 % of views.

## How the numbers were taken
- Skill `ig-viral` (method: outlier = views ÷ the channel's median of its last 40 Shorts).
- Its helper `swipe.py` is not shipped with the skill, so the ranking was done with a small script in the same way.
- **YouTube Shorts only.** Instagram and TikTok need a login or the user's own browser, and this cloud session has neither (the skill forbids logging in). YouTube counts are public, so the skill names it as the second corpus.
- Data: `yt-dlp` (public channel lists, view/like/comment counts, upload dates, top comments).
- Video and thumbnail hosts are blocked in this session, so the videos could not be watched. **Beats were rebuilt from the title, description and the top comments**, which quote the key lines. Marked "beats from comments" below.
- Corpus: ~120 channels seen in searches, 66 channel medians computed, ~2,400 Shorts ranked.

## What the winners share
- All 4 used ones are **relationship humour or warmth with one clear twist at the end** ("wait for end", "the ending").
- 3 of 4 are under 70 s with **one line of dialogue that people quote back in the comments** (Kevin's lines). That line is the hook formula: **"The Calm Reply"** (one partner tests, the other answers with a calm one-liner).
- The top one (342x) has no words at all: an act of care, then a funny twist.
- Sample: 8 passing Shorts from 5 channels. Enough to pick remakes, not enough to call a trend.

## Mapping (original beat → our stickman beat)

**Reel 32 "Wait For It"** ← #1 Scary Day, "Cute couple, wait for end" (beats from comments: in a forest he brings a purse he found and does her makeup; "it doesn't matter whose makeup, he just wants to see her happy"; "unexpected ending").
- He finds a lost purse → he finds a little paint box.
- He does her makeup, clumsy brush → he paints her face, clumsy brush strokes.
- She looks happy → she looks in a puddle mirror: crooked lines, big red cheeks.
- Unexpected ending → twist: she laughs, takes the brush and paints him even worse. Both laugh.
- Text: "HE HAD NO IDEA HOW. / HE JUST WANTED YOU TO SMILE."

**Reel 33 "Let Her Go"** ← #2 Havefunzer, "No point in being jealous" (beats from comments: she tests him to make him jealous and says she's leaving, he stays calm: "Want me to order you an Uber?", "Why would I hold someone who wants to leave?", "After all your fake tests I don't even have the energy to be jealous").
- Jealousy tests → she walks off holding a rope tied to his wrist and looks back, waiting for the pull. 3 tests.
- Calm reply → he calmly unties the rope and holds the end out to her.
- Twist → the rope falls, she stops, runs back and holds his hand instead.
- Text: "I DON'T HOLD / WHO WANTS TO LEAVE."

**Reel 34 "3 AM Question"** ← #5 Havefunzer, "They can never get enough" (beats from comments: she wakes him at 3 AM to ask "do you still love me?"-style questions; punchline "we're already testing that hypothesis").
- 3 AM wake-ups → clock 03:00, 03:14, 03:41; she pokes him awake, heart-question bubble.
- He answers each time → he draws a heart in the air, half asleep, falls back.
- Punchline → last poke: he pulls the blanket over both of them and hugs her.
- Text: "WOULD YOU LOVE ME IF I WAS ANNOYING? / WE'RE TESTING IT RIGHT NOW."

**Reel 35 "Not A Monster"** ← #6 Aniblague, "Greatest healer's son & Human can change" (beats from title and series: the one everybody fears turns out good; people can change).
- Feared figure → dark spiky stickman, the crowd steps back.
- He changes → each step he helps someone, a spike falls off, his color turns light.
- Ending → the crowd that ran now walks up to him.
- Text: "PEOPLE CAN CHANGE. / IF YOU LET THEM."
