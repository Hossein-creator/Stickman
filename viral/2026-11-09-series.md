## The story: THE YELLOW UMBRELLA
Same couple in every part (him: white; her: white with a bun), her strict DAD (grey, hat, moustache), a RICH SUITOR (gold, tie). Object that ties it together: his yellow umbrella.

**Part 1/4 "The Umbrella"** (hook: lightning, she's stuck in the rain)
- Storm at the bus stop, she has no umbrella. He arrives with a yellow one, gives it to her, runs off into the rain.
- Next day she waits to give it back; he arrives sneezing. Rain again: one umbrella, two people. Every day after: they walk together.
- Cliffhanger: her dad sees them. "WHO IS HE?!" → "PART 2 → FOLLOW".

**Part 2/4 "The Window"** (recap, hook: dad drags her away)
- Dad: "STAY AWAY FROM MY DAUGHTER!" / "YOU HAVE NOTHING!"
- Night: she's locked in; he stands under her window in the rain every night, paper plane "I WILL WAIT"; she sends a heart back.
- Dad finds the note and tears it. A week later a rich man brings a ring; dad shakes his hand.
- Cliffhanger: "THE WEDDING IS TOMORROW." → "WILL SHE MARRY HIM?"

**Part 3/4 "The Wedding"** (recap, hook: wedding bells, ring box opens)
- He runs through the storm to stop it; the wind breaks his umbrella, he falls in a puddle, gets up.
- He bursts into the wedding, soaked, holding the broken yellow umbrella. Suitor laughs, dad throws him out.
- The ring comes closer to her finger … cliffhanger: "DID SHE SAY YES?"

**Part 4/4 "The Choice"** (recap, hook: the ring falls, "NO.")
- She runs out in her veil. He is at the bus stop with a suitcase; the bus leaves; she runs after it and falls to her knees. "TOO LATE?"
- The rain stops falling on her: he got off the bus and holds the broken umbrella over her. Hug, sun, rainbow.
- Dad arrives, holds his umbrella over both and nods.
- "5 YEARS LATER": the three of them (+ a kid) under the yellow umbrella at the bus stop.
- Card: "REAL LOVE DOESN'T NEED A RING. IT NEEDS SOMEONE WHO STAYS IN THE RAIN." "THE END".
