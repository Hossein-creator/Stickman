## How the numbers were taken
- Method of the skill `ig-viral`: outlier = views ÷ the channel's median of its last 40 Shorts.
- **YouTube only** (Instagram/TikTok need a login; not allowed). `yt-dlp` flat lists: 15 relevance searches (388 channels) + 25 searches filtered to **this month, sorted by views** (YouTube filter `sp=CAMSAggE`), channel medians for ~450 channels.
- Single-video pages were blocked this run (YouTube bot check), so **dates and likes come from each channel's public RSS feed** (`/feeds/videos.xml`, has publish date, views and likes for the last 15 uploads). Comment counts were not available, so engagement = likes only (a lower bound).
- Videos/thumbnails can't be watched here. **Beats rebuilt from title + the creator's description.**
