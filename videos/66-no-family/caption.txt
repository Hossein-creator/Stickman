Age 9: he stood alone in the rain outside the orphanage.
A girl walked up, held her umbrella over him and gave him half her sandwich.
15 years later he waited in front of a little house with a ring: "Be my family?"
"YES!"

He had no family. So she became one.

"The bond that links your true family is not one of blood, but of respect and joy in each other's life." – Richard Bach
