## Intent

Reel 2 of the stickman mindset account. Theme: doer vs overthinker / self-made limits.

shakes the bars, sits down. DOER walks to the door, pulls the padlock: it was never closed, it drops.
He pushes the door open and walks out into the light.
Punchline: "THE LOCK WAS NEVER CLOSED." / "ONLY ONE OF THEM CHECKED."
