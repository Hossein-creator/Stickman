At 25 he had the gold car, the champagne and a crowd around him.
At 26 the money was gone. So was the crowd.
One friend walked over, lit a small fire and sat down next to him.
At 80 they are still sitting by that fire.

Money brings a crowd. Hard times show you who stays.
